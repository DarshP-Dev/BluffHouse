# Bluff House — Original Full Vision

Preserved from the learner's supplied concept. This is future product context, not approved MVP scope; see scope.md and roadmap.md.

# PROJECT OVERVIEW

**Name:** Bluff House

**Full-game tagline:** "Trust Nobody. Bet Everything. Become the House." Preserved from the product interview; the MVP uses "Trust Nobody. Call Their Bluff."

**Concept:** A real-time multiplayer Blackjack party game that combines traditional casino gameplay with social deception, bluffing, betrayal, strategic alliances, minigame duels, and player-controlled casino mechanics.

**Core philosophy:** Players shouldn't win solely because they have the best Blackjack hand. They should also be able to outsmart, challenge, bluff, sabotage, and negotiate with their friends.

Bluff House is an entertainment-focused social game using entirely fictional virtual chips, with no real-money gambling, deposits, purchases of wagerable chips, or cash redemption.

# COMPLETE GAMEPLAY VISION

## 1. Multiplayer Blackjack

Blackjack is the primary game.

- Everyone begins with virtual chips.
- Players compete at a shared Blackjack table.
- Players can hit, stand, double down, and place wagers.
- A live leaderboard displays current chip balances.
- Gameplay is synchronized between players.

## 2. Become the House

Players compete to control the casino through a hidden auction.

- At the beginning of a game, players submit sealed bids using their starting chips.
- Nobody sees other players' bids until the auction ends.
- The highest bidder becomes the House.
- If the highest bids are tied, randomly select the winner among tied bidders.
- The winning bid contributes to the Vault.
- The House earns a small, balanced commission from Blackjack winnings.

After the House has served at least one round, the other players can initiate an overthrow vote.

- At least 75% of eligible non-House players must vote in favour.
- The system randomly selects an eligible challenger.
- The challenger and current House play a fast 1v1 duel.
- The duel winner becomes or remains the House.

The House must not be able to manipulate the actual random card draws.

## 3. Fast-Paced 1v1 Duels

Players can challenge each other for virtual chips.

- The challenged player chooses the minigame.
- Both players commit their wager before the game.
- Every duel should last approximately one minute or less.
- Winner receives the duel pot.
- Other players can spectate.

Possible games:
- Rock Paper Scissors
- Connect 4
- Bullet Chess
- Coin Flip

Duels are also used for House overthrows and bounty challenges.

## 4. Call My Bluff

Players can challenge claims made by other players during Blackjack.

For example, someone claims they can hit and still win. Another player can wager against that claim.

The system settles the challenge based on the actual outcome.

This feature should create player-to-player betting, tension, and opportunities to deceive opponents.

## 5. Bounty Board

Players can place and contribute virtual chips toward bounties on other players.

- Anyone can add chips to an active bounty.
- The bounty target chooses the duel minigame.
- The targeted player must win three consecutive bounty challenges to claim the bounty themselves.
- If the target loses, the challenger receives most of the bounty, with a smaller portion going into the Vault.
- Introduce safeguards against challenge spam.

## 6. Deals and Betrayals

Two players can enter a temporary agreement by committing chips.

Each secretly chooses either **Split** or **Steal**.

- Split + Split: Both receive their shares and a small cooperation bonus.
- Split + Steal: The player choosing Steal takes the pot.
- Steal + Split: Same outcome in reverse.
- Steal + Steal: The pot goes to the Vault.

## 7. Chaos Cards

Chaos Cards introduce unpredictable gameplay effects.

At the end of eligible rounds, the bottom two chip positions receive Chaos Cards. Everyone tied at either qualifying position also receives cards.

Potential cards:
- Double or triple an eligible wager/pot.
- Swap Blackjack hands with another player.
- Rotate everyone's hands one position left.
- Discard a recently drawn card and redraw.
- Receive bonus virtual chips.
- Activate special betting conditions.

Chaos Cards should have clear activation windows, limitations, and balanced effects.

## 8. The Vault Finale

The Vault grows throughout the match through auctions, designated contributions, penalties, and other mechanics.

At the end, players enter a special Blackjack showdown.

The finale introduces **Shadow Draw**:

- Players can draw cards that remain hidden from their opponents.
- They can choose whether to reveal the cards or make claims about their hand.
- Other players, including the House, can call their bluff.
- Successfully bluffing produces a reward.
- Getting caught produces a penalty.
- Incorrectly calling a truthful bluff also has consequences, potentially including revealing the challenger's own hidden cards.

The Vault gives losing players a potential comeback opportunity.

## 9. Multiplayer Platform Features

The full platform should eventually include:

- Host private rooms with join codes.
- Public Quick Play matchmaking.
- Customizable player profiles and avatars.
- Unlockable achievement badges.
- Match and lifetime leaderboards.
- In-game text chat.
- Optional open-mic voice communication.
- Spectator views for duels and major events.
