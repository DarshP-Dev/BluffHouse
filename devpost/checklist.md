## Approved Party-Mode Rules

These resolve expansion decisions with the learner's explicit corrections and approval. All amounts are fictional safe-integer chips. These choices are not changes to Classic Blackjack.

### Phase boundaries and economy

- Classic stays three rounds, 1,000 starting chips, wagers 50–250 by 50, 20-second betting/decisions, eight-second bluff offers, and the approved payouts and recovery rules. Required emoji and system announcements ship with the core, even though the extended prompt also lists them among platform features.
- Party initially uses three regular rounds. Sequence: House auction → betting/optional Chaos windows → normal Blackjack/bluff → settlement/summary → bounded social interval → next round; after the last round, finish outstanding obligations then run the Vault finale when enabled. No social event overlaps a live Blackjack decision, accepted bluff, or reveal. Social intervals last 20 seconds for admission; at most one accepted room-wide contest extends an interval, with no further invitations in that slot after it. Invitation and ballot deadlines are capped by the interval's close; an unaccepted offer or unsuccessful ballot cannot extend it. A threshold-reaching overthrow reserves the current admission slot while the defender chooses. There is no social interval after the final regular round. Initially verify one contest per interval; slice 10 extends six-round Party to two sequential contests. Each slot allows up to 20 seconds admission, 8 seconds defender selection when required, 60 seconds play, and 2 seconds result display. The complete two-slot social phase has an immutable 180-second maximum (90 for one slot). Unused first admission ends the interval; after a completed first contest a second admission can open only with enough time for its worst-case full deadline. Never run contests concurrently. Cancel incompatible unaccepted offers on commitment, cap ballots/invites to admission close, and reject any start without sufficient remaining phase budget. Bounty qualification stays once per target per interval. Record a tested one-slot fallback and the concrete risk if two slots cannot be safely verified; do not silently remove the approved attempt.
- Keep separate typed reservations, Blackjack commitments, duel/deal/bounty escrows, Vault balance, finite bonus reserve, and immutable settlement records. With `B` = player uncommitted balances (reservations included), `E` = live committed escrow, `V` = Vault, `R` = remaining bonus reserve, and `D` = cumulative net dealer funding, assert `sum(B) + E + V + R = initial player chips + initial bonus reserve + D`. Every fee or bonus has a source and destination. No fee is deducted twice, and reserved funds are never counted twice.
- Party starts a finite bonus reserve of 100 chips per starting participant, separately disclosed from player starting stacks. Emergency Fund and cooperation bonuses consume it. Blackjack returns and capped House commissions are the explicit dealer bank sources/sinks; no invented peer payout or arbitrary top-up is allowed. Unused bonus reserve is not player winnings.
- Until the interactive Vault finale is built, Party completion distributes its funded Vault evenly to all original match participants, including absent/departed players. Integer remainders go in original seat order. The same fallback applies when the finale is disabled or the match ends early. Refund unresolved bounty contributions to their original contributors; release unaccepted offers; settle accepted contests by their published deadline rules before results. No money is discarded by cleanup or replay.
- Party inherits the core low-player recovery and affordability checks at safe boundaries. Do not start a new contest/round below two connected players. Confirmed early termination drains existing obligations before final standings; it never starts a fresh finale to delay closure.

### House auction, commission, and overthrow

- Twenty-second sealed auction before the first betting phase. A bid is 0 (pass) or 50–300 in steps of 50; revise/withdraw before close. Even the maximum bid preserves 700 starting chips. Show submission status and each player's own bid, never another amount before reveal. Submitted bids remain binding through disconnect. Highest positive bid wins; secure random selection breaks highest ties. All-zero bids create no House. Release losing reservations; transfer the winning bid once to the Vault and reveal all bids.
- The House is separate from the neutral dealer and room host. The fictional dealer bank pays `floor(3 * eligible positive Blackjack profit / 100)` as an additional commission, never a deduction from the winner. Cap combined House commissions at 50 chips per completed regular Blackjack round, allocating the remaining cap in settled player-seat order. No commission on pushes, losses, the House's own hand, peer challenges, duels, bounties, bonuses, or Vault payouts. Display player payout and separate bank-funded commission. A 100-chip normal win returns 200 to its player and pays 3 extra bank chips to the House; a 50-chip natural returns 125 and pays 2 additional bank chips to the House. Record commission as explicit net dealer funding D with its own once-only settlement identity. Classic payout rules remain unchanged.
- A House must hold the role for one completed regular round before an overthrow ballot. One ballot per social interval; at most one Last Stand. Freeze eligible connected, non-departed non-House voters at ballot opening. Threshold is `ceil(0.75 * eligible voters)`; denominator does not shrink after disconnects. Vote once, ten-second deadline. Choose randomly among affirmative voters still connected at resolution; if none remain, no duel starts. A failed ballot does not reopen that interval.
- Last Stand uses the shared duel engine with zero chip stakes, so insolvency cannot make the role permanent. The defending House chooses a released minigame within eight seconds; a selection timeout forfeits the role to the selected challenger. Win retains/transfers the crown; draw retains it. Ordinary duel disconnect/deadline rules apply. After transfer, tenure restarts and another completed regular round is needed. House powers never influence card randomness or host permissions.

### Direct duels and minigames

- During a social interval, one outgoing offer per player; 50–250 matched stake by 50, eight-second invitation deadline. Reserve inviter funds; the defender selects a released game as part of acceptance. Acceptance validates both funds and commits the pot atomically. Rejection/expiry releases the offer. First accepted room event cancels conflicting unaccepted offers. Full winner pot, tied outcome refunds each stake; all settlement is once-only.
- A duel has an absolute 60-second play deadline after acceptance, independent of browser animation. Disconnect never refunds an accepted losing stake: play clocks continue; a player's failed required action forfeits, both players failing the same simultaneous decision draws. Reconnection restores current private/public state. Explicit resignation forfeits. Event summaries fit within normal bounded transitions.
- Rock Paper Scissors: five simultaneous throws, eight seconds per throw; one locked hidden choice per player each throw. First to three wins ends early; ties consume a throw; after five throws the greater win count wins, equal counts draw. One missing choice loses that throw, both missing draw it. This bounds even repeated ties.
- Coin Flip: defender selects heads/tails in acceptance; one cryptographically random server flip awards the pot immediately. No client seed or winner field.
- Connect 4: standard 7-column by 6-row gravity board; server validates turns and four-in-a-row/full-board draw. Server randomly assigns first turn. Eight-second move deadline forfeits to the opponent; hard total play limit 45 seconds. An unfinished board at the hard limit draws with refunds. Show both timers so the cap is explicit.
- Bullet Chess: standard initial position, legal moves and promotion through a maintained rules library verified at implementation time; 30 seconds per side, no increment, plus a 60-second wall-clock cap. Server validates checkmate, stalemate, repetition/other library-supported draws, resignation, and flag fall. Flag fall wins for the opponent unless that opponent has no possible mating material under the implemented documented rule; timeout-material cases must be tested and must not be guessed from a library's global insufficient-material test. A hard-cap unresolved position draws. No illegal move changes state or pauses a clock. If correct rules/library support cannot be verified, do not expose chess as finished; retain the unchecked work and explain the blocker.

### Chaos distribution and effects

- Award after settled non-final regular rounds, before the social interval. Sort all non-departed match participants by balance; everybody with balance at or below the second-lowest position's value receives one random released card (ties included). Temporarily absent participants remain eligible. Hold at most two; at capacity, skip the grant and announce inventory full. One activated card per owner per round/hand; an opt-in effect recipient also consumes that round's activation allowance. Reveal grants/activations publicly; the available registry and rules are visible.
- Double Down Chaos and Triple Down Chaos activate only in betting on an existing reservation: raise that stake to 2× or 3× its original amount, cap 750, funded immediately and subject to availability. They do not secretly multiply a challenge pot. Natural profits stay exact because stakes remain multiples of 50. Withdrawing/replacing the affected wager removes the multiplier; it does not refund the consumed card or activation. Commission uses the resulting actual net Blackjack profit.
- Pot Twist opens one five-second opt-in window after ordinary betting closes but before commitment/dealing. Only already submitted wagers can opt in to double, funded before commitment, capped at 750, with no stacking with another multiplier. Activation is announced, acceptance is voluntary, and deadline closes it once. A failed/unfunded action consumes nothing. The name describes a special higher-stakes round opportunity; no unfunded pot is created.
- Emergency Fund gives up to 100 chips from the remaining bonus reserve, once per player per match, during betting before submitting a wager. If the reserve is empty the card stays held and the action is unavailable with a reason. Any smaller remaining award is shown before activation. This is a disclosed Party comeback mechanic, never a Classic rebuy.
- Hand Swap and Table Turn use a single five-second window after initial natural checks and before any ordinary player decision. Only connected players with funded, non-natural, unacted hands participate; the neutral dealer and natural hands never move. Swap exchanges two eligible two-card hands; rotation moves all eligible hands one seat left. Wagers, seat order, ownership of the one-offer allowance, and account records stay with seats. Only one hand-movement effect can resolve in this window; later attempts fail without consuming a card. A dealer natural skips the window entirely.
- Second Chance is available to the active player only after their most recent ordinary Hit leaves a live total below 21, before their next decision. It replaces that one drawn card using the existing shoe, preserving the discarded card's consumed status. It cannot undo a bust, 21, natural, accepted challenged draw, settled result, or any protected reveal. Update the decision ID and reset the playable timer once; reject stale activations. No card changes an accepted bluff's historical truth.

### Bounties and Split or Steal

- During social admission, contribute 50–250 by 50 against a different eligible participant, capped at 1,000 total per active target bounty. Contributions commit immediately to escrow. One active bounty per target; no self-funding, cancellation refund, or diversion to another target. Contributions cannot change during an accepted bounty duel.
- A bounty invitation uses a matched 50-chip duel and normal defender choice/acceptance; one qualifying bounty defense per target per interval. Allow creation only when at least three social intervals, counting the current one, remain before the finale. With no final-round social interval, three-round Party cannot support three defenses; enable a six-round Party preset in this slice so the complete streak is playable. Display remaining opportunities before funding. Other contests may consume those intervals; unused bounty funds follow the ending refund rule.
- A target win pays the normal duel pot and increments its defense streak; three consecutive qualifying wins award the full bounty once. A drawn duel refunds duel stakes and leaves the streak unchanged. A target loss pays the duel pot normally, then `floor(85 * bounty / 100)` to the challenger and the exact remainder to the Vault; close the bounty and reset its streak. Refused/expired invitations do not count. Disconnect never cancels an accepted contest. Unresolved bounty escrow at any match ending refunds each contribution before rankings.
- Split or Steal: equal contributions of 50–250 by 50, one room event at a time. Inviter reserves; acceptance commits both. Each locks a private choice within ten seconds. Both Split returns each contribution plus up to 10 bonus chips per player from the finite reserve; pay equal integer bonuses, leaving an odd reserve chip unused. One Steal takes the full funded pot; both Steal send the pot to the Vault. A missing choice defaults to Steal, clearly disclosed before acceptance. Disconnect does not cancel an accepted contract or reveal a locked choice early. At most one accepted contract per player pair per match prevents repeated bonus farming.

### Vault finale and Shadow Draw

- Runs once after normal Party rounds and outstanding settlements, with at least two connected eligible players and a nonempty funded Vault. No additional buy-in is required, allowing low-chip comebacks. Snapshot eligible connected, non-departed match participants at finale entry; later disconnection does not remove their locked entry. Late reconnecting non-entrants can watch. Otherwise use the announced equal-share fallback.
- Use a fresh server-shuffled six-deck shoe. Each entrant gets two cards visible privately to themselves; opponents see only card backs/count. In seat order each gets one ten-second decision to Stand or take exactly one hidden Shadow Draw, then their entry locks. Timeout stands. A voluntary reveal exposes the full locked hand permanently. No dealer bet or normal Blackjack payout occurs in this contest.
- Complete every entrant's draw/stand stage before starting claims in seat order. A player may voluntarily reveal or declare one specific total from 17 through 21, regardless of their real total, including a bust. Ten seconds to declare/pass, then an eight-second first-valid-challenger window. House players can challenge under the same eligibility rules. One challenger per claim, no self-call, no chip requirement; server knows truth privately. Claims freeze the hand and have once-only identities.
- Unchallenged claim: use the declared total as the effective Vault base total plus one successful-claim point. A false unchallenged claim remains eligible even with a truly busted hand. Challenged exact truth: reveal claimant, use real total plus two claim points, penalize the incorrect challenger two contest points and reveal that challenger's hidden hand. Challenged false claim: reveal and disqualify claimant; award challenger two contest points with no penalty. Voluntary reveal exposes cards permanently; no claim scores the actual total with no claim bonus, and an actual bust without a successful unchallenged claim is ineligible.
- At settlement, combine each eligible entrant's effective base total, claim bonus, and accumulated challenge points/penalties. Highest effective score wins the funded Vault; ties split evenly with remainder in original seat order. Reveal all cards at settlement, but do not retroactively invalidate an unchallenged accepted lie. If everyone is disqualified/ineligible, distribute equally to all original participants. Pay once, drain Vault, then rank overall match by final chips. Disconnect does not undo locked entry/results; intentional departure after entry retains the already-started contest's obligations.

- Public/private snapshot tests must prove no hidden values, totals, derived eligibility, shoe order, or truth labels leak before a reveal. All entrants can reach the draw/claim stage without funding; recovery, early-ending drain, and zero-pot paths are explicitly tested. The future **Vault Thief** badge requires a false unchallenged claim that actually changed the winner set in the player's favor, not just any Vault win.

Reject incompatible settings on the server. Quick Play joins a compatible public lobby with capacity or creates one atomically; never admits to an active match or silently changes an existing private membership.
- Optional Party Blackjack extensions: first add ordinary Double Down (initial two-card non-natural hand, before any offer/hit/Chaos, fund one equal extra stake, one draw then Stand), and a second immediate claim **"My next card gives me exactly 21"** using the same hard 12–20 eligibility, one-offer-per-hand limit, matched stakes, forced single draw, expiry, and reveal rules. The main game already provides seated observer views of each released duel/event. Unverified individual Chaos effects/minigames remain visibly unavailable in rules documentation and cannot be selected as functional options.
