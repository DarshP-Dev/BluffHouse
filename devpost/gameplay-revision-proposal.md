## A. House bankroll and dealer gameplay

### Approved model: finite Table Bank + personal first-loss bond

The House controls the dealer hand and risks personal chips. A separate, finite Table Bank supplies payout capacity so a high auction bid does not make the dealer unusable. Bank backing is explicitly new initial fictional funding, not a loan the player must repay and not player wealth.

| Item | Proposed exact rule |
| --- | --- |
| Auction | Sealed bids 50–900, step 50. Winning bid moves entirely to the existing funded Vault; losing bids return. |
| Table Bank | Allocate **2 × the sum of original participant starting stacks**, once at Party match start. Its initial amount is `R0`. Keep a separate ledger, public available/committed balance and no automatic top-ups. |
| Personal bond | Winner commits `min(250, available personal chips after auction payment)` to a separate House bond. A legal winning bid must leave at least one chip to bond. The bond remains personal wealth but cannot also fund bets, duels, claims or bounties. |
| Payout capacity | Uncommitted Table Bank + current House bond back every accepted Blackjack wager. Vault, bonus reserve and other players' escrows never collateralize these liabilities. |
| House earnings | After each settled round, dealer profit restores any Table Bank shortfall below `R0` first. Split the remaining profit **50% House / 50% Table Bank**, with odd chip to the Bank. House earnings first restore its bond to 250; excess becomes available personal chips. |
| House losses | Round net dealer losses consume the House bond first, then the Table Bank. No negative balance, debt or collection from unrelated personal reservations. The auction payment is already at risk in the Vault and is not returned as dealer capital. |

**900-bid example:** four guests start with 1,000 each. The Table Bank starts at 8,000. The winner pays 900 into the Vault and bonds their remaining 100. The dealer can cover all three opponents going All-In for 1,000: maximum combined profit liability is 4,500, fully below 8,100. If those bets all lose normally, 3,000 dealer profit splits 1,500/1,500; 150 restores the House bond to 250 and 1,350 becomes spendable. If all three instead win naturally, the House loses its 100 bond and the Bank pays the remaining 4,400. The 900 Vault payment remains separate in both cases.

### Exact betting and settlement

1. Accepting wager `w` reserves the bettor's stake and **`floor(3*w/2)` of dealer liability**, atomically. Capacity cannot be reused by another bet. Stake refunds/edits also update the paired liability reservation atomically. Display the current backed maximum and rejections clearly.
2. On a loss the bettor's stake becomes dealer proceeds. An ordinary win returns stake plus `w` profit; a genuine two-card natural returns stake plus `floor(3*w/2)` profit; a push returns stake only. Settlement does not count a returned stake as dealer expense.
3. Determine all hand outcomes first. Let `net = losing stakes - winning profits`. Apply the round's **net** to bond/Bank using the table above, in one idempotent batch. This avoids changing the House's earnings merely by reordering seats. Collateral already covers the gross worst case, so no losing wager is needed to fund an advertised winner.
4. All-In always uses the bettor's actual available chips. It is accepted only if its maximum profit is currently backed. Opening normal-stack All-Ins fit the proposed Bank; **later All-Ins are not guaranteed** after stacks grow or the Bank loses capital. Show the affordable maximum; reject the whole request rather than silently reducing it. The finite Bank can shrink and cannot promise unlimited later bets.
5. Ordinary Double Down and Chaos multipliers require both additional player funding and additional dealer collateral before changing state or consuming a card. Existing Chaos 750-chip multiplied-wager cap stays. Hand Swap/Table Turn affect only eligible bettor hands; the House hand stays fixed. Second Chance stays a bettor-only effect. Emergency Fund uses only the existing finite bonus reserve.
6. Existing additional claims remain separate matched-player escrows, never Bank-backed side bets. The House cannot make claims requiring an ordinary bettor hand. Social duels, deals and bounties continue to use personal available chips only. Dealer bond and Bank principal are excluded from standings profit; owned bond and personal dealer earnings are included exactly once.

### Dealer decisions, transfers and failure cases

- The House sits in the dealer seat and receives the actual dealer hand, not a second ordinary hand. The owner sees its hole card. Opponents receive only the upcard until dealer resolution. After bettor turns, **Hit below 21 or Stand at any live total** are legal; a natural/bust resolves automatically. The server draws, checks legality and owns RNG/shoe. On decision timeout or temporary disconnect, finish with the existing stand-on-all-17s policy. No manual shuffle or card selection.
- If the bond reaches zero, the House may continue using the remaining finite Bank; subsequent earnings rebuild its personal bond before becoming spendable. This is limited liability with an expensive auction, not an unlimited personal guarantee. Public UI shows bond at risk, spendable chips, Bank backing, committed liabilities and actual dealer profit/loss separately.
- If capacity is insufficient, reject additional wagers/multipliers without changing existing commitments. If there is no capacity for even a one-chip bet at a round boundary, close the match safely: settle/refund outstanding obligations, then use the funded early-ending Vault fallback. No automatic replenishment. A final zero-wager Shadow round may run only if B is approved and normal participant/time constraints allow it.
- Nobody bids: the same already allocated Bank supports a server-controlled casino dealer, with no personal bond or player profit share. All guests remain bettors. All dealer net profit/loss belongs to the Bank. Classic retains its currently verified automatic-dealer rules.
- Overthrow/Last Stand occur only between settled Blackjack rounds. Keep the existing ballot, selection and zero-stake contest rules. On a successful crown transfer, release the outgoing House's remaining bond once; the incoming connected player posts `min(250, available)` before the next betting phase. **A penniless winner can take the crown with a zero bond**, but its first dealer earnings are locked into the bond before cash becomes spendable. The same depleted Bank stays with the table; a transfer never creates capital. A defense/draw retains the current bond. Host ownership remains separate.
- Temporary disconnect never cancels a funded hand or releases liability. Explicit departure completes the current round by automation, settles the bond once, then uses the same finite casino Bank next round until a lawful crown transfer. Returned bond remains on the departed participant's retained match ledger. No rejoining to avoid losses.
- On replay, finish every obligation first, then create the next match's openly declared starting funding. Never refill the current match through replay, reconnection or repeated commands. Store unique IDs for bond entry/release, liability reservations, net settlement and profit credits.

## B. Shadow Blackjack and the funded Vault

### One normal Blackjack round with hidden cards and accepted bluffs

1. After configured ordinary rounds, refund unresolved bounties using the existing rule, then open one final Shadow betting phase. The original current participants, including zero-chip entrants, receive **two cards each**, including the House's one dealer hand. First card is public; all subsequent cards and real totals are owner-private until a legal reveal. A casino dealer has one public upcard and one hidden card. Original participants with zero wager may compete for the Vault; no unfunded Blackjack profit is promised.
2. Every bettor may make one funded normal wager, subject to A's collateral checks. The House posts no opposing bettor wager. Use ordinary card values, soft aces and genuine two-card natural detection. Hold automatic natural/bust settlement until the bluff process finishes. The genuine private result must not leak through public status, action flags or balance changes.
3. Bettors act in original seat order, then the House. Each gets **one configured decision-time budget**, not a fresh full budget after every Hit. They may repeatedly **Hit** while their real hand is below 21 and not busted, **Claim 21** immediately, **Draw & Claim 21** atomically if another draw is legal, or **Declare True Total & Stand**. One claim maximum per hand; a claim or truthful declaration ends that hand's turn. A hidden bust may still claim 21 or reveal its real bust, but cannot draw again. A true non-21 declaration immediately reveals the complete hand and locks its actual total; no call window.
4. Each Claim 21 opens exactly **eight seconds**, pausing ordinary turn progression. All other connected original entrants who are not Vault-disqualified may call, **including players with zero available chips or an All-In wager**. No call bond, fee or stake is required. The first valid server-accepted caller resolves the window atomically. Each entrant may call at most once in the finale. Calling must not inspect or reveal any hidden information before acceptance.

### Consequences and funded rewards

| Event | Blackjack result | Personal transfer and Vault eligibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Correctly call a false 21 | Reveal claimant. A bettor's wager loses to dealer; a lying dealer is treated as busted, so surviving opposing hands win from reserved backing. | Pay caller `min(25, remaining Vault)` as a funded call reward. Claimant cannot win the remaining Vault. |
| Incorrectly call a real 21 | Reveal claimant; truthful total remains 21, including genuine-natural status. Caller retains its legitimate Blackjack hand/wager. | The incorrect caller **loses all eligibility for the remaining Vault**, reveals their complete hand and must play truthfully thereafter. No chip transfer is demanded. The truthful claimant is explicitly vindicated, keeps 21 (and genuine-natural status), remains Vault-eligible and receives normal funded Blackjack settlement. |
| Nobody calls by the exact deadline | Accept 21 as the effective total **even if the true hand busted**. It can win against the dealer; an accepted false dealer 21 can defeat sub-21 bettor hands. | No call fee/reward. Claimant stays Vault-eligible. Reveal the actual hand at final showdown without undoing the successful bluff. |

A genuine initial natural receives 3:2 only when it actually qualifies. An accepted bluff receives ordinary 1:1; it cannot invent a natural. A correct caller's reward can be smaller if the Vault is nearly empty and may be zero if empty. Display the current capped funded reward and the wrong-call loss of Vault eligibility before calling. A truthfully challenged claimant receives no invented bonus; vindication preserves their hand and jackpot opportunity while the caller loses theirs. Zero-chip and fully committed All-In entrants have the same claim/call permissions as funded entrants.

5. A wrong caller whose hand is revealed has its future hand forced truthful: it can continue legal Hits on its own turn, then declare its real total; it cannot claim a different accepted 21. Already completed hands keep their valid effective Blackjack result, but lose Vault eligibility. Caught-liar hands end immediately. Multiple windows never overlap; a deadline-expired call fails without consuming money/allowance. A called claim never reopens.
6. The player House uses the same hidden-hand Hit/Claim/Draw & Claim/true-declaration rules on its dealer turn. Its first card is public. A truthful dealer timeout follows automatic stand-on-17 using its remaining decision budget, then reveals. The casino fallback never bluffs and follows automatic 17. A caught dealer is a bust for funded Blackjack, not a magical cancellation of liabilities. The House may compete for the Vault with its one dealer hand and may call earlier claims without paying a fee, subject to the same once-only allowance and wrong-call consequences.
7. On a bettor timeout or disconnect, reveal/stand truthfully at the actual current total; no auto-bluff. An already open claim retains its full existing window even if its owner disconnects. Reconnect restores exactly the same private hand and deadlines. Explicit departures receive no new calls/claims; their already funded wager and accepted declaration still settle, and original-participant Vault rights follow their resolved eligibility. Zero-wager entrants have identical hand/eligibility rules. One entrant's departure never redistributes committed money early.

### Final settlement, no-claim case and fallback

8. Resolve the funded Blackjack wagers using effective accepted totals, real declared totals and actual natural status. Then settle House net/bond accounting under A. Accepted 21s remain binding even if showdown reveals a bust. Correct-call rewards already debited from the Vault are not paid twice.
9. The **highest eligible effective non-busted total** wins the remaining Vault. If nobody claims 21, this is ordinary highest live Blackjack total, including the House. Genuine naturals and accepted 21s tie at 21. Split equal winning totals evenly, giving whole-chip remainders in original seat order. This makes an uncalled lie a direct route to the jackpot rather than a point bonus. Final showdown reveals every hand and labels actual total, declared/effective total, successful bluff, caught lie and wrong call separately.
10. If no eligible live hand remains but some entrants were not call-disqualified, split the remainder equally among those non-disqualified original entrants, even if their true hands busted. **If every original entrant is disqualified, split the remainder equally among all original entrants as the explicit last-resort funded fallback.** This preserves a complete payout of the funded Vault; in that exceptional case caught liars can receive a fallback share, but their lost wagers and lost winning eligibility remain final. If this exception is unwanted, it needs a different explicitly approved destination, not silent destruction of the Vault.
11. If the finale is disabled or the match genuinely ends before the first Shadow card is dealt, retain the existing original-participant equal-share Vault fallback. Once dealt, an early-ending condition truthfully completes unresolved hands by automation, closes each already-open call window at its original deadline, settles funded wagers, then applies rules 9–10. No new financial admission after termination confirmation. If no dealer capacity remains, only zero-wager hands can enter Shadow, and the Vault remains the funded prize. No eligible connected participants means automated resolution, never a stranded pot.
12. Store immutable finale/hand/claim/call/transfer IDs and a single finalization record. Check phase, owner, turn, deadline, funds and prior resolution before any mutation. All errors are projection-safe. Public snapshots/events/reconnect payloads must omit concealed rank/suit/IDs, true total, natural/bust status and outcome hints until legitimate reveal. Private actions may reveal legality only to their owner. The finite shoe is server-owned; no client chooses cards or supplies totals. Settlement must conserve every ledger and remain once-only under retries and concurrent callers.
