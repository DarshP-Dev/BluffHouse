# Runtime and rules map

One Express/Socket.IO process serves the compiled React app, same-origin APIs and authenticated commands. Development adds a Vite proxy, never a second game authority. Production uses secure randomness; injected clocks/shoes are test-constructor dependencies only.

1. `apps/server/src/app.ts` issues an opaque HttpOnly guest cookie, checks the exact configured origin and grants control only to the newest socket. Credentials stay out of snapshots and client storage.
2. `apps/server/src/rooms.ts` validates context, caches request fingerprints and reconciles deadlines. A synchronous candidate transition checks authority, rules and invariants before replacing one room root and incrementing its revision. Retries return their original result.
3. `apps/server/src/game/` owns Blackjack, claims, contests, Chaos, House, bounties, deals and Shadow Blackjack. Commitments have funded sources, owned escrow and once-only settlement; animations cannot draw or pay.
4. `apps/server/src/projection.ts` explicitly builds viewer snapshots. Dealer holes, bids, simultaneous choices, private Shadow hands/shoes and claim proofs remain hidden as required.
5. `apps/web/src/connection.ts` follows snapshots, rejects stale revisions, disables offline sends and reconciles uncertain acknowledgements without replaying speculative actions. Chat has a separate acknowledgement lane so a pending message cannot block Hit/Stand.
6. `apps/web/src/App.tsx` presents the illustrated lounge, three-column lobby, sealed auction stage and responsive dashboard. `reference-theme.css` is imported after the existing component styles; local artwork and its prompts are documented in `ui-artwork.md`. The October 25 gameplay stylesheet loads after the reference theme and keeps the large felt and every seated hand first. Contextual decisions and live evidence follow without moving the table. `BetBuilder.tsx` maintains only a local additive chip draft; the server still authorizes all wagers. `HostControls.tsx` submits validated lobby configuration and `ChessDuelBoard.tsx` renders authoritative clock interpolation. A single chat portal keeps its actual composer/history mounted while its container moves between layout slots, preserving focus, draft, reading position and unread tracking across phase changes.

Party conservation is `balances + committed escrow + Table Bank + Vault + remaining bonus reserve = initial player chips + initial Bank + initial bonus reserve`. Reservations remain inside personal balance; the House bond is counted once. `house-bank.ts` pairs every live bettor wager with gross maximum-profit backing from the finite Bank and standing bond. A separate mandatory dealer bet reserves10 when affordable, can increase up to spendable chips, and commits its own escrow before cards. It never expands bettor admission capacity. Round net losses consume dealer stake, bond, then Bank. Profit restores Bank shortfall, pays up to the personal stake from eligible residual profit to the House, then splits the remaining residual50/50 (odd chip to Bank). Unlost stake principal returns separately; earnings refill the bond before becoming spendable. `dealerNet = initialBank-currentBank` is derived, never extra funding. Classic retains its bankless invariant.

The House occupies only the dealer hand, never a second bettor hand. Owner-only projection reveals its hole card to that player; other players receive only the upcard until resolution. Explicit `dealer:hit`/`dealer:stand` commands check owner, phase, decision identity, deadline and legal total. Timeout/disconnect automation follows stand-on-all-17s. Reconnect flapping never extends an existing automated deadline. Crown changes release only the outgoing bond reservation and establish the incoming bond, retaining the same Bank. Results release remaining bonds without crediting their already-owned principal again.

Delayed claims hold two stakes without drawing; actual round settlement evaluates the contract, pays once, then grants Chaos and advances. Unresolved bounties refund before finale entry/ranking. Accepted contests drain during early termination. Shadow Blackjack has a separate server shoe and unique final round/betting/hand IDs. `shadow.ts` handles its single-budget turns, binding21 claims and free eight-second call windows. `vault.ts` compares effective Blackjack hands, validates real-natural3:2 payouts and awards the funded remaining Vault after the House Bank net. Calls consume at most25 already-funded Vault chips; disqualifications never create a debt. Projection masks every concealed card/total and private action outside its owner until legitimate reveal, including the base room fields. Replay retains the room/chat/session profile and resets match funds, obligations and statistics.

Demo safeguards: 20 rooms, six seats per room, 2,000 guests, 4KiB incoming payloads, 30 commands/second/guest, 60 entry requests/minute/IP, 60 system events and 100 chat messages per room, two Chaos cards and eight badges per guest, and a 100,000-record request-memory admission cap. Match limits bound contest histories; room cleanup releases request/archive records. These are not measured concurrency guarantees. See the final report for observed load/payload evidence.

Production imports no test fixture or experiment and exposes no clock, draw, voice-signaling or test-control API. Audio experiments remain test-only. Fonts are bundled through `@fontsource/cormorant-garamond`, with the OFL at `apps/web/public/fonts/OFL.txt`; pinned `chess.js` has its recorded BSD license. No external font CDN, API key or paid service is required.

Lobby-only kicks remove membership and notify the removed controller without touching active obligations. Room-owned win records increment once at final settlement and survive replay/guest rejoin until that room ends.

R4 accounting and the R5 private-card Blackjack replacement implement the separately approved rules in `devpost/gameplay-revision-proposal.md`. The original point-scoring finale remains recoverable at the R4 checkpoint; active R5 has no contest points. Pre-deal termination refunds reservations and uses the original equal-share Vault fallback; after dealing, unresolved hands finish truthfully while any already-open call keeps its original deadline. Ledger finalization is once-only and conserves Bank, personal balances, escrows, Vault and bonus reserve.
